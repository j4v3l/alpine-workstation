# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from feedback.
- Protect private information and report security concerns through the documented channel.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
